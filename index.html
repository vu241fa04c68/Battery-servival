HOW TO PLAY

FIELD GUIDE

01 · MOVE

Use WASD or the ARROW KEYS to move around the station. Stay mobile when Stalkers arrive.

02 · BATTERIES

Collect yellow batteries to refill your signal. Your battery count is shown in the top-right HUD.

03 · BOOSTERS

Stand close to a circular pad and press E. A deployed booster slows signal drain in that area.

04 · SIGNAL PULSE

Press SPACE to push nearby Stalkers away. The pulse costs signal and needs time to recharge.

05 · SURVIVE

Dark zones drain signal faster. Stalker hits also reduce signal. Keep signal above 0% for 90 seconds.

06 · LEVELS

Levels start at 30 seconds and add 10 seconds per level. Complete the previous two levels to unlock the next one.

07 · WIN / LOSE

Reach zero signal and the transmission is lost. Survive the full timer to hold the signal and win.

08 · EXIT

Press the ← BACK button at the top of the game to leave the current run and return to the main menu.
